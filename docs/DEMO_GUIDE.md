# Demonstration and recovery guide

Use fictional information. Before replacing an open interview, choose **More options → Save copy**. Recorded answers remain in the page; download a copy to retain them independently.

## A short walkthrough

1. **Welcome → See an example.** Point out **Example · Everything here is fictional**. This prepared sample does not establish live interview quality or verified company practice.
2. **Interview → Transcript.** Read an answer and follow-up; use **Find**. Open a topic in **Workspace → Notes** to show its answer, topic guidance and sources. On a narrow screen, open **Workspace** first.
3. **Workflow → a step → a source.** Select a diagram step, read its details, then open a cited source. **Back to what you were reviewing** restores the previous view. Show **Fit**, the step selector and **Follow path** to explain a handoff or branch.
4. **Review.** Visit **Report**, **Notes**, **Workflow**, **Steps**, **Items**, **Variations** and **Open questions**. Edit the example under **Steps**; point out **DRAFT · NEEDS OWNER REVIEW**. In a real interview, **Create draft** opens this surface. **Update draft** uses newer answers and replaces manual draft edits after confirmation—save first.
5. **Save and export.** **Save copy** downloads the restorable interview. **Transcript → Export** offers Markdown or JSON for review, not restoration. Review provides report **Download .md**, **Download notes**, and Workflow **Download** for SVG.
6. **Optional live segment.** Choose **Start your own → Type instead** for a fictional exchange. Use **Start talking** after the device checks below. Stop when useful; do not invent details to reach completion.

## Explain what the team and topic states mean

There is one interviewer voice. **Lead interviewer** handles the conversation; **Coverage reviewer** and **Process reviewer** inspect completion readiness. **Document analyst** handles supplied material. **Standing by** is normal when a role is not needed. **Done** means work finished, not necessarily that the account passed.

**Unknown** and **In progress** mean the section still has gaps. **Captured · awaiting review** is provisional. A topic is officially covered only when its explanation and required details have evidence, with no unresolved assumptions. An unavailable answer remains open; a partial draft can still be useful. Participant confirmation and process-owner approval are separate.

When the candidate account is ready, one acknowledgement precedes the completion review; in voice it finishes playing first. Review can produce another question, a final omissions check, or a partial handoff. Confirmation cannot fill missing sections. Retrying the same failed review does not repeat its acknowledgement. **Wrap up** remains unavailable until its prerequisites are met.

## Recover without losing the account

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Typed completion review is taking too long | **Pause review** preserves recorded answers and unsent text. **Resume review** starts with fresh review authority; paused results cannot close the account later. |
| Voice needs to stop | **Stop talking** ends capture and playback. **Mute mic** is temporary. Use **Type instead** to continue. |
| An interview or completion request fails | Use the displayed **Try again** or **Retry completion review**. Do not resend the same answer as a new message. |
| A file needs attention | In **Assets**, use **Retry upload**, **Retry review**, **Retry source** or **Retry action**, as offered. **Continue without waiting** returns to the interview with file work unresolved. |
| A file needs a purpose or is for later | Enter what to look for and choose **Review now**, or **Save for later**. **Revisit this file** brings a parked file back for review. |
| Facts or sources change | Add the correction and inspect its source. Adding, removing or revisiting files changes the evidence basis. Continue interviewing or use **Recheck coverage** when offered. Earlier completion does not apply automatically. An **Initial overview** preserves earlier reading; current review counts and limitations are shown separately. |
| Reopening saved work | **More options → Open saved interview…** accepts a Save copy file. Answers and sources remain, but previous coverage/closure is provisional until rechecked. Opening alone does not request a model response. |

## Hands-on device check before a voice demonstration

These physical checks remain to be performed on the presentation device:

- [ ] In **Settings**, choose the intended voice and **Preview voice**. Confirm the actual headset/speaker route, intelligibility and volume; test **Stop voice**.
- [ ] Start talking, wait for **Listening**, and speak a short fictional answer. Confirm the transcript, the next question and natural pause handling.
- [ ] Check **Mute mic → Unmute mic**, then **Stop talking** during playback. Confirm no further sound or capture and that typing remains usable.
- [ ] Check that the interviewer does not capture its own speaker output in the intended room/device arrangement.
- [ ] Verify microphone-denied recovery to typing and **Save copy → Open saved interview…** with a disposable fictional session.

The November 2026 checks used real Chrome for the UI, controlled fictional responses for broad recovery scenarios, and actual voice/transcription services for a synthetic-audio roundtrip. Native browser playback and recording were exercised; Chrome output was muted and no physical microphone was used. Those results do not establish physical audio quality, live process accuracy, final release readiness or a latency guarantee.
