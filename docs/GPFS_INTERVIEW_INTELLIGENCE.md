# GPFS interview intelligence — 0.26.1

Implemented November 5, 2026 in the canonical Process Collector checkout.

The interviewer learns entity or enterprise scope, applicability, the participant's
basis for a standard, and boundaries through a natural walkthrough. These are
coverage requirements rather than an opening questionnaire. Explicitly unavailable
details and standards are parked rather than repeatedly demanded.
Reported team practice is distinct from an approved GPFS standard. Enterprise
HR, IT and internal work remain supported.

The shared rubric contains **16 topics**: the original eleven plus scope, rules,
connections, data/systems and volume/effort. Completion still requires the current
independent coverage and process reviews. Older eleven-topic captures acquire
five unknown topics and cannot retain completion authority for the new rubric.

`gpfs_interview.py` owns the shared context, selection guidance, conditional modules,
classification vocabulary and metadata contracts. Modules cover fund, GP/carry,
ManCo, multiple entities, calculations, spreadsheets, client approval and actual
client differences. A client name alone is not a variant. Upstream calculations
are dependencies unless their actual performance is within the interview boundary.

Rule capture distinguishes source, interpretation and implementation, including
calculation inputs, sequence, dimensions, dates, prior periods, rounding, overrides
and corrections. Checks distinguish review levels/objectives, client review from
blocking approval, thresholds, segregation, escalation and enforcement. Variations
retain charging/removal fields and add class, source, nature and normal-step link.

Background taxonomy transactions have exact authorized citations, controlled
facet/value pairs, correction targets and source fingerprints. Rewritten or removed
sources retire their tags and dependencies. A single interview cannot establish a
gold standard: local classification requires participant testimony plus independent
documentary corroboration. This is still not process-owner approval or legal verification.
Legacy defaults apply only to recognized old capture, draft and step structures;
they cannot supply missing required fields in unrelated evidence/permission schemas.

Draft steps add control type, data origin, entry mode, rule source, entity scope and
parent process. Reports, memo/detail exports, saved interviews and the knowledge
detail view preserve these fields and cited external process relationships. Internal
workflow edges remain distinct from reported external dependency leads. New graph
proposal types are subprocess_of, parallel_with and shares_calculation_with; the
existing counterpart-evidence and review gates remain in place.

Short recaps at useful points in the walkthrough are correction checkpoints. They confer no
completion authority. The spoken front retains the exact shared question-selection
policy and a compact sixteen-topic guide; detailed coverage tables stay in background
capture/review. Ordinary live follow-ups still use GPT-Live directly without a new
synchronous question-review call. Silent progress updates carry current coverage
labels only, without a suggested question, module script or mid-answer interruption.
They remain bounded to one small context event. No model, credential, package or
runtime change was required.
