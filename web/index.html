              FROM CONVERSATION TO CLARITY

              How this works

              A useful process document starts with your account of how the work gets done.

              1. 01

                Tell the story

                Talk or type about a process. Describe what starts the work, who does it and how you know it is complete. Add files when they help.

              2. 02

                Connect the details

                Check Notes and Workflow as they update. Select a source to review the original answer or file excerpt. Captured topics stay provisional until Wrap up.

              3. 03

                Review it together

                Choose Wrap up, answer the final question, then create and review the draft. A process owner checks the procedure before approval.

              Keep a copy of your work
