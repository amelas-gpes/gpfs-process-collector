              Recovery copy

              Off unless you turn it on. Keeps one local copy of your transcript, notes, map, draft and unsent answer. Never recordings.

              How recovery copies are cleared

              The copy is written a few seconds after a change. It stays on this computer and is offered when the app next opens. It is deleted after a confirmed Save copy, when you start over or open another interview, when you discard it, when you turn this off, and after seven days. Save copy is still the way to keep an interview long term.

              How this works
              A useful process document starts with your account of how the work gets done.
              
          
              1. 01
                Tell the story
                Talk or type about a process. Describe what starts the work, who does it and how you know it is complete. Add files when they help.
              2. 02
                Connect the details
                A topic circle fills when the notes capture an answer or you confirm why the topic doesn’t apply. Each filled circle adds one segment to Topic coverage. Open any circle to see the evidence and what still needs an answer.
              3. 03
                Review it together
                Choose Wrap up to check the account. You can add details, confirm the final omissions question when it appears, or save a partial draft for follow-up.
              
          
              Definition of done
              
            
              The interview is done when someone can follow the work from its trigger to its finish, the app has reviewed all 16 topics and the participant confirms the account. The process owner then reviews it for approval.
              
            
              Keep asking when an answer could change how someone performs, checks, releases or closes the work. For a client variation, establish what changes. For an overview, identify which detailed process owns the finish.
              
            
              Hand a question to the owner when the participant explains the affected work, what can proceed or must wait, and who or what can resolve it. One method may stay on hold. Another route may allow closure with an exception if someone records it and takes responsibility.
              
            
              Keep a partial interview if a material gap leaves the route or its hold unclear. Open a topic in Notes to see its gaps and follow-ups. You can save the interview and return with someone who knows the missing detail.
